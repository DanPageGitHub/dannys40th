Tent camping

Tent camping is calculated separately from the event donation: £15 per adult per night, £7.50 per child aged 5+ per night, and free for under-5s.

If you book tent camping through this form, Danny will collect the camping money from you and forward that camping money to the venue. It is not part of the event fund.

You can also pay the venue for camping when you get there and only send Danny the event-fund donation beforehand. The venue are easy-going, and the extra camping field is large, so it should not sell out before the event.

Untick this if you plan to pay the venue directly for camping when you arrive.

You can also book camping yourself on the Barge Inn Honey Street camping page, book closer to the time, stay more nights, come a night earlier, or just sort camping when you get there. When you arrive, tell the venue you're with Danny's 40th and they'll point you in the right direction.

We are not camping in the main field. There will be quiet camping and a Naughty Corner for louder camping. No kids in the Naughty Corner, but it is all the same field.
