Tent camping payment

Tent camping is calculated separately from the ticket/event-fund donation: £15 per adult per night, £7.50 per child aged 5+ per night, and free for under-5s.

If you pay camping through this form, Danny will send that tent camping money to the venue. If you pay the venue directly, your camping cost is still logged for headcount, but it is not added to the amount you pay Danny now.
