Sacred Geometry

Five mandalas are always present, each built on a different sacred symmetry: 5-fold (the pentagram), 6-fold (the hexagram), 7-fold (rare — found in Sufi art and quasicrystal geometry), 8-fold (the octagram of ancient cosmology), and 12-fold (the zodiac, the hours, the chromatic scale). Each form independently cycles through phases of emergence, full presence and dissolution — never quite in sync, so the composition is never the same twice.

The concentric rings within each mandala follow the golden ratio φ ≈ 1.618, spacing at 1, 0.786, 0.618, 0.5, 0.382, 0.236 and 0.146 of the full radius — the same sequence found in nautilus shells, pine cones, the spiral of galaxies, and the proportions of the human body.

Each frame, two counter-rotating layers are drawn per mandala — one at full opacity, one at 45% — creating depth without a 3D engine. Arms spiral outward using parametric arc placement; the apparent complexity comes from a single formula iterated across each symmetry axis.

In Sound mode the microphone feeds the Web Audio FFT. Bass frequencies drive lateral sway and speed; treble drives line density. A beat fires when any band crosses 1.65× its rolling average, sending a visual spike with a 180 ms cooldown — the equivalent of a kick-drum hit rendered in light.
