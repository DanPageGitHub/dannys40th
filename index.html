How we got here

Yes, I know. I already had a 40th.

That one was a lovely surprise — genuinely, not a word of complaint. But I've been insisting for years that I want a birthday party where it isn't absolutely hammering it down or bloody freezing.

If you've got a December birthday, you know exactly what I mean. Summer birthday lot — you don't know when you were born.

So here we are. A weekend at The Barge Inn on the Kennet & Avon canal, in the height of summer, with good people and no excuses. The plan is deliberately loose in places, but the spirit is firmly: something a bit magical. Something that'll feel worth the wait.

I'd love to see as many of you there as possible. Please fill in the form so I can get the date locked in.

Where We're Going

The Barge Inn, Honey Street

A canal-side pub built in 1810, deep in the Vale of Pewsey. It sits on the Duke's Ley Line running between Avebury and Stonehenge, with the Alton Barnes White Horse cut into the hill directly opposite and Adam's Grave long barrow on the ridge above. The crop circle room inside has a painted ceiling described as the Sistine Chapel of Wiltshire. There is nowhere quite like it.
