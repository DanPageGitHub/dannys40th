Yes, I know. I turned 40 in February...

Some of you will know there was already a do. It was an incredibly lovely surprise and I can't express my gratitude enough to all those involved in putting it on. But you know what I'm like - I love throwing parties. And I've always wanted to do a birthday celebration where we might actually get some sunshine, with camping.

If you're unlucky enough to have a birthday in the deep of winter, you'll know exactly what I mean. Summer birthday lot: you don't know when you were born.

So I'm planning a do at the Barge Inn. It's a special place where I've celebrated a lot of very important moments, so it feels only right to return for this one. Right now I'm largely getting a vague idea of numbers and seeing who's up for coming along, helping me organise or playing some music etc., so fill in the form to let me know.

Where We're Going

The Barge Inn, Honey Street

A legendary canal-side pub built in 1810, tucked away in the beautiful Vale of Pewsey, The Barge Inn sits right on the Duke's Ley Line, an ancient alignment said to run between Avebury and Stonehenge.

With the Alton Barnes White Horse carved into the hillside directly opposite, it's hard not to feel like you've stumbled upon somewhere steeped in a touch of magic.
