Yes, I know. I turned 40 in February...

Some of you will know there was already a do. It was an incredibly lovely surprise, and to all those that put it on: I cannot express my gratitude enough. But you know what I'm like - I love throwing parties. And I've always wanted to have a birthday thing where we might actually get some sunshine, with camping.

If you're unlucky enough to have a birthday in the deep of winter, you'll know exactly what I mean. Summer birthday lot: you don't know when you were born.

So I'm planning a shindig at the Barge Inn. It's a special place where I've celebrated a lot of very important moments with equally important people, so it feels only right to return for this one.

Tickets can now be booked, so please grab yours and fill in the form to let me know who’s coming, who’s up for helping organise, playing some music, or getting involved etc.

Where We're Going

The Barge Inn, Honey Street

A legendary canal-side pub built in 1810, tucked away in the beautiful Vale of Pewsey, The Barge Inn sits right on the Duke's Ley Line, an ancient alignment said to run between Avebury and Stonehenge.

With the Alton Barnes White Horse carved into the hillside directly opposite and the Crop Circle Museum nextdoor, it's hard not to feel like you've stumbled upon somewhere steeped in a touch of magic.

Celebrating something? Birthday in July?

If you'd like to make this a joint do, let me know! I'm well up for joining forces. Let's have an awkward chat about changing the URL.

Getting There

The Barge Inn is at Honey Street, Pewsey, SN9 5PS, off the B3087. The lane down to the pub is single-track, so take it steady. When you arrive, speak to the people in the check-in hut and they’ll point you to the right place.

By Car

Off the B3087, parking on site. Main what3words for arrival: ///remake.adults.rave

By Train

Pewsey is the nearest station, about 10 minutes by taxi. Flag in the form if you need a lift coordinating.

By Narrowboat

It's right on the Kennet & Avon. If you have access to a boat, this is your moment.

Car Pool
