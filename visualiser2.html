About

This started as about twenty lines of canvas code tucked behind the party page - a vague shimmer of rotating shapes that you would barely notice while reading about camping prices. Then one late evening became several, the controls multiplied, the gyroscope got involved, and it quietly became its own thing. It runs entirely in your browser; no library, no framework, just maths and a canvas element that got a little out of hand.

Technology

Viz2 is built with HTML5 Canvas 2D and a single requestAnimationFrame draw loop. Audio comes from the Web Audio API: an AnalyserNode with FFT for frequency bands, getUserMedia for the microphone, and getDisplayMedia for tab or screen capture so you can visualise another tab’s playback. On mobile, DeviceOrientation drives gyro parallax. Presets and auto-save use localStorage. Vanilla JavaScript, no frameworks or libraries.

Sacred Geometry

Five mandalas are always present, each built on a different sacred symmetry: 5-fold (the pentagram), 6-fold (the hexagram), 7-fold (rare - found in Sufi and Islamic geometric art and quasicrystal geometry), 8-fold (the octagram of ancient cosmology), and 12-fold (the zodiac, the hours, the chromatic scale). Each form independently cycles through phases of emergence, full presence and dissolution - never quite in sync, so the composition is never the same twice.

The concentric rings within each mandala follow the golden ratio φ ≈ 1.618, spacing at 1, 0.786, 0.618, 0.5, 0.382, 0.236 and 0.146 of the full radius - the same sequence found in nautilus shells, pine cones, the spiral of galaxies, and the proportions of the human body.

Each frame, two counter-rotating layers are drawn per mandala - one at full opacity, one at 45% - creating depth without a 3D engine. When Sound is on, the microphone (or captured tab) feeds the Web Audio FFT. You can choose which band (Bass, Mid, Treble) drives speed, sway, or density in the Mic routing grid. A beat fires when a band crosses its rolling average, sending a visual spike.
