Costs

Camping is £15 per adult per night, £5 for under-16s, free for under-5s paid directly to the venue. There are also glamping bell tents (£90/night or £160 for the weekend) and a bed and breakfast option in the pub.

I may do tickets or ask for a separate contribution towards costs, but I'm still figuring that out, and I don't want cost to be a reason anyone can't come.
