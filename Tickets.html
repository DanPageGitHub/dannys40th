Schedule Updates

Friday night

There is a night on at the pub if people fancy it, which should be decent. Otherwise, we can settle in properly with some bevvies, outdoor games with Greg and a general first-night-social sort of mood. We’ll be doing a bring-a-dish shared campsite meal at 8pm.

Saturday day

If you’re arriving on Saturday, get there nice and early. Duncan from Altogether Games will be running easy-to-play silly fun stuff, there’ll be interactive installations from Danny and others in the barn, Aran has built some wicked controller-based multiplayer interactive breakcore machines, and we’re planning an audio electronics jam that anyone can get involved with.

Saturday night

DJs will involve Anorak, Salander, S.Murk, The Panger, Algorithmic, Myr, Gizmode & Breakwhore, The Mighty Rick, Golgot, and maybe a few more if we can squeeze them in. If you’re not on the list here and you think you should be, pester Danny (as he might just not know your DJ name or have had full confirmation you’re coming). If you’re on the list and you can’t make it anymore, let Danny know!

Sunday

Roast at the Barge, a trip to nearby beautiful Avebury for a wander round the stones for those who can find the energy and recover from Saturday night. Sunday night: cinema time in the barn. Stoopid B-movies and BangFace TV-style madness.

Staying over

Campers: If you’re camping in your own tent you can book here and I’ll forward on your details and the cash straight to the Barge Inn, or you can choose to pay later directly to the campsite.

Glamping: If you’d like to go for glamping, please book that directly with the Barge Inn Honey Street, as this form doesn’t reserve those spots. Limited spots are available so book quickly if you wanna live it up in style.

Vans: If you’re bringing a van, campervan, or motorhome, and if you need an electric hookup, please book directly with the venue. Those spaces are limited, so book quickly.

Please still fill out this form either way, even if you’ve already booked or paid the venue, so I know who’s coming and when people are staying.

Tent camping payment

Tent camping is calculated separately from the ticket/event-fund donation: £15 per adult per night, £7.50 per child aged 5+ per night, and free for under-5s.

If you choose to pay tent camping through this form, I’ll collect that camping money and send it on to the venue. If you’d rather pay the venue yourself, that’s fine too — I’ll still use your answers here for the group headcount.

Expected nights

Please answer each night, even if you're not sure. This is for headcount and camping maths. “Not sure” is logged but not charged as camping now.

You’re welcome to come earlier, stay longer, or decide about extra nights when you’re there. Just be honest about what you end up doing so Danny doesn’t undercount the group.

Event Fund - Per-person Donation

£20 each is suggested to help cover event costs. More is massively appreciated, but less — or £0 — is completely fine if you can't afford it.

Food plan

Friday evening is a bring a dish shared campsite meal at 8pm. There is also a night on at the pub if people fancy it, otherwise we can settle in with drinks, hang out around the campsite and the pub itself, and get stuck into Greg’s outdoor games.

Saturday daytime matters too, not just the night. If you’re only coming for Saturday, get there nice and early for Duncan from Altogether Games running easy-to-play silly games, interactive installations in the barn, Aran’s multiplayer breakcore machines, and an audio electronics jam for anyone who wants to join in. DJs will kick off later on.

Sunday plan is a roast at The Barge, a wander over to Avebury to see the stones and have a proper mooch, and cinema night later on. The Barge’s head chef is vegan, so there should be loads of options at the carvery.

Breakfast can be bought onsite or at the nearby affordable cafe. If enough money is raised, Danny will try to cover making breakfast for at least one morning.
